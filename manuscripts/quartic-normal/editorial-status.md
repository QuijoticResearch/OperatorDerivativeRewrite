# Quartic normal equations: editorial status

Reviewed draft, October 29, 2026. Mathematical baseline `bab688bb6140cd6316c80680095a914ea0d346db`.

The paper reconstructs full proofs from exact source definitions and declaration bodies. It covers the entire continuous dual for arbitrary measures, with a complete real normed source and a positive lower bound on the bounded linear L4 operator. It includes the optimal exponent at zero, weighted source regularity, finite directions, absence of weighted coercivity at every Poisson source, and optimal exponent at every Poisson dual datum.

The energy derivative uses the dimension-independent declaration in the older finite-dimensional module; existence uses the complete-space module. The distinction has been checked. No Lp dual-representation theorem is assumed. The theorem called an equivalence is a nonlinear bijection. The finite-direction result concerns source differences in one fixed subspace. The weighted estimate compares a fixed datum to all other data and is not stated as a pairwise neighborhood Lipschitz theorem.
