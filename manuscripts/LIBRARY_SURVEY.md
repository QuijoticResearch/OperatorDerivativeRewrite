## Detailed assessments retained outside standalone papers

The generic sticky generator is a dense closable C0 differential relation with a maximum-principle bound. The range condition and closed-semigroup identification remain open in that source contract. The boundary martingale core does not discharge them. Initial-kernel nonuniqueness concerns versions on unobserved states, rather than nonuniqueness of the sticky martingale problem. The completed sticky-clock paper has a narrower, independent construction and observed-martingale scope.

The mixed Gaussian/Dirac full-support law has a well-defined smooth derivative that is not closable; a pure Dirac law fails representative independence. The continuous observation t-positive-part times a Gaussian coordinate has instantaneous raw information arrival. These elementary obstructions remain supporting diagnostics rather than separate papers.

The fully inspected PoissonLpLowerObstruction, PoissonLpTwoRegime, StableLevyAnnuli and StablePoissonRecovery modules show classical small/large intensity count-norm regimes and failure of recovery in a prescribed plain product Lp input norm. Their stable-mark application assumes finite-set count marginal laws and does not construct a Poisson random measure or stable process. Dirksen's primary 2014 Theorem 1.1 already explains the need for sum/intersection input norms. This is supporting analysis, not activation of queued stable-process research.

The additionally inspected exponential Volterra family gives the classical OU prediction defect, integrating-factor martingale and original Bochner-L2 drift compensation. Its power-kernel construction is Riemann–Liouville and is not standard fBM merely by variance normalization. A bounded predictable delayed sine coefficient has a random nonconstant integrated drift, including under dominating laws. These remain supporting checks, without importing random-kernel stochastic Fubini, sample-path semimartingale identification or a new standalone Volterra paper.

The inspected finite signed, Fourier, gauge and fluid supporting families are not promoted from their names or module counts. A finite frequency energy calculation, growing zero-free window, local gauge gap, conditional spectral bridge, exact triad identity or restricted-Euler trajectory does not solve its corresponding unrestricted analytic problem.
