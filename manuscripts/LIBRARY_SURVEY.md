## Coverage and limits

The [machine inventory](LIBRARY_SURVEY.json) covers every one of the 2,795 Foundation Lean modules (359,781 source lines) in this frozen checkout. For each module it records its byte hash, imports, available module description, named declaration headers and an editorial subject tag. Tags based on prefixes are navigation aids, not significance scores. This is a complete module-level inventory; it is not a claim that every proof has received a new human review. The existing full verification receipt remains the authority for the baseline build and complete transitive axiom audit. The root/import files and Basic.lean add navigation or scaffolding, while the two Legacy Lean files are historical material outside this Foundation publication universe.

Detailed definition, hypothesis, proof and literature review is performed for selected paper candidates. Fourteen papers now have complete human proof expositions, exact source maps and matching reviewed PDFs. A large source family or declaration count is not evidence of novelty or a reason to produce a standalone paper.

Ordinary weak probability convergence must be distinguished from restricted analytic-transform convergence when assessing the root-walk candidate.

## Additional detailed family assessment

The sticky generator closure is explicitly a dense closable C0 differential relation with a maximum-principle bound; its module states that a range condition and closed-semigroup identification are not assumed. The boundary martingale core is not silently promoted to that missing identification. The initial-kernel nonuniqueness example only changes versions on unobserved states, not the sticky martingale problem. These are retained as supporting diagnostics, not a new standalone process paper.

The mixed atomic derivative example supplies a regular full-support Gaussian/Dirac law with a well-defined smooth derivative that is not closable, while a pure Dirac law fails even representative independence. The continuous observation t-positive-part times one Gaussian coordinate has a raw filtration with instantaneous information arrival. Both are useful elementary counterexamples, not selected as separate new research papers.

Countable stochastic-convolution modes give exact orthogonal trace criteria; damped exponential modes have the classical reciprocal-rate trace condition and quadratic-versus-linear rate examples. Gaussian terminal Hermite-rank prediction has an exact energy series and leading-rank remainder, with fractional small-time lower obstructions. These sources merit a consolidated Gaussian prediction/forcing exposition only after a distinct contribution and complete comparison are established; their large infrastructure counts are not novelty evidence.
