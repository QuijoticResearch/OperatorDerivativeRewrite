## Coverage and limits

The [machine inventory](LIBRARY_SURVEY.json) covers every one of the 2,795 Foundation Lean modules (359,781 source lines) in this frozen checkout. For each module it records its byte hash, imports, available module description, named declaration headers and an editorial subject tag. Tags based on prefixes are navigation aids, not significance scores. This is a complete module-level inventory; it is not a claim that every proof has received a new human review. The existing full verification receipt remains the authority for the baseline build and complete transitive axiom audit. The root/import files and Basic.lean add navigation or scaffolding, while the two Legacy Lean files are historical material outside this Foundation publication universe.

Detailed definition, hypothesis, proof and literature review is performed for selected paper candidates. Nine papers now have complete human proof expositions, exact source maps and matching reviewed PDFs. A large source family or declaration count is not evidence of novelty or a reason to produce a standalone paper.

## Mathematical assessment

| Story | Assessment and publication decision |
|---|---|
| Gaussian–cubic truncated moments | Newly selected eighth paper. Exact normalized square-test thresholds, spectral realization through eight moments, strict nine-moment criterion and null-boundary failure form one coherent finite moment story. The general moment machinery is classical; priority for the explicit parameter formulas remains unresolved. |
| Arithmetic entire functions | Three completed papers cover fractional endpoint/nonreal-zero behavior, the full quartic eventual threshold/collision, and sixth-order eventual real/simple zeros. Keep actual integral, time, order and zero quantifiers separate. No A(2)/RH or general integer-order conclusion is available from these papers. |
| Signed higher heat | The completed signed multichannel paper already joins factorial analytic domains, native coefficient limits and completed observable reconstruction. The wider band-limited, signed-kernel and root-channel infrastructure is supporting material until an independent additional mechanism is identified. |
| Complex-root and marked renewal approximations | Exact holomorphic transform limits coexist with positive-law non-tightness, and exponential tilts admit a single fixed bounded continuous test with diverging integral/variation. This is a meaningful quantitative comparison candidate, but Bonaccorsi–Mazzucchi already distinguish their analytic-test limits from ordinary weak convergence. Do not manufacture a novel general obstruction from that classical distinction. |
| Actual IBM | Spatial quadrature has a complete companion. Strong remaining candidate: original adjoint-graph-dual time differentiation, positive-time native nonrepresentability and norm escape on each fixed raw observed past. The necessary original clock-domain proof and observed-filtration limits must be reconstructed before another paper is claimed. Existing hidden-clock notes remain separately scoped. |
| Cauchy/Poisson circle flow and entropy | Now the ninth reviewed paper: full explicit circle-kernel/Feller, closed generator/resolvent, smoothed core, forward Bregman production and nonsharp initial-time entropy decay proofs. Tanaka’s known pole dynamics are credited. Positive Lipschitz energy and continuous-function graph domains are retained. This is not certification of the different queued original Cauchy manuscript. |
| Gaussian prediction, fractional processes and Wick responses | Large reusable positive-law library, including actual continuous observations, full-past conditional projection, higher-chaos prediction, countable forcing trace criteria and positive-time roughness diagnostics. Much of its central structure is standard Gaussian Hilbert-space theory. Consolidate exact domain/trace/obstruction applications instead of writing papers from infrastructure counts. |
| Brownian clocks and sticky constructions | Actual random clocks, occupation and conditional two-time operators are useful. The inspected two-time positivity/constant-preservation module explicitly does not imply Chapman–Kolmogorov or stationarity. A full process/kernel paper needs its complete final hypotheses and classical sticky-diffusion comparison; no full Markov claim is inferred from its filename. |
| Banach and closed operators | The quartic normal-map paper already extracts a coherent existence/sharp-regularity story. Generic adjoint, closure, energy resolvent and graph-dual results mainly support applications. Atomic full-support nonclosability and observation-filtration counterexamples may support a consolidated diagnostic paper after primary-literature review. |
| Fourier covariance and periodic semigroups | The completed exposition connects classical Schoenberg structure to the actual Haar-L2 cone, generator domain and sharp active-axis threshold, with separate counterexamples. Later periodic/nonlocal entropy material should be assessed through exact domains rather than advertised as diffusion curvature. |
| Finite fluid geometry and diagnostics | Finite Fourier, real Beltrami, pressure/trace cancellations, cone/triad geometry and corridor classifiers require separation of exact algebraic diagnostics from estimates for unrestricted nonlinear PDE solutions. Source-level breadth alone does not justify a Navier–Stokes regularity or singularity paper. Select a distinct proved diagnostic only after auditing its actual field class and averages. |
| Finite gauge and one-sided block geometry | Exact incidence/gauge ranks, boundary Schur forms, rational gap certificates and finite positive kernels are substantial finite mathematics. The active researcher's Yang–Mills campaign retains priority. Do not chase later commits or convert a local finite gap, conditional spectral bridge or block error budget into a scale/volume-uniform continuum mass gap. No new gauge paper is selected here. |

## Primary comparisons newly checked

Curto and Fialkow, *Recursiveness, Positivity, and Truncated Moment Problems*, Houston J. Math. 17 (1991), 603–635: [primary article](https://www.math.uh.edu/~hjm/v017n4/0603CURTO.pdf). The introduction and Theorems 3.1/3.9 were inspected. Their general truncated Hamburger criteria govern the classical context of the new moment paper. The explicit formula search is preliminary and is not evidence of novelty or an exhaustive negative search.

Bonaccorsi and Mazzucchi, *High Order Heat-type Equations and Random Walks on the Complex Plane*, [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6140): the construction and analytic-limit context were inspected. Ordinary weak probability convergence must be distinguished from restricted analytic-transform convergence when assessing the root-walk candidate.
