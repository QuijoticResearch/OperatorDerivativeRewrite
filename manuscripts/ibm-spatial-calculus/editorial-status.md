# IBM spatial Simpson calculus: editorial status

Reviewed companion exposition, October 29, 2026. Eight pages. Mathematical baseline `bab688bb6140cd6316c80680095a914ea0d346db`.

This paper deliberately focuses on spatial Simpson quadrature, nonseparable deterministic time-space chains, uniform common-grid prefixes, bounded measurable random horizons for spatial one-forms, and explicit Bernstein continuous-path realization. It provides complete human proofs. The previous six-page `ibm-signed-simpson` note is preserved, including its separate raw-channel and graph-dual results. Those results are not silently imported as quadrature premises or claimed to have been fully reconstructed in this companion.

The human proof uses the direct conditional Gaussian calculation `E|Z_b-Z_a|^4 = 3 E(||B_b|-|B_a||^2) <= 3(b-a)` to obtain quartic tightness. The stronger verified limit to 3T is not needed, and is not inferred from an expectation bound. This is a proved expository reorganization of the existing convergence result, not a new Lean theorem claimed under the same shorter argument. The exact formal declarations use their established quartic/Peano infrastructure.

The finite approximation is defined from the one-form and oriented spatial endpoints. The spatial midpoint is distinguished from a physical-time midpoint. The compact remainder has a vanishing fourth-order modulus under C3 one-form regularity; no fourth derivative of the one-form or fifth derivative of its primitive is assumed. All localization, probability-product and time-integral steps are proved. One common prefix bound controls the entire grid without a growing union bound. The Bernstein construction is explicit and its positive basis controls the supremum error without a grid-cardinality factor.

The time-space theorem keeps C4 spatial slices, C1 time slices, and jointly continuous time partial and fourth spatial derivative. Joint continuity of F is kept explicitly for the continuous-path source correspondence. Random horizons do not require stopping times. Bernstein smoothing is not asserted causal; the targets are not identified with an intrinsic divergence, and no arbitrary predictable integrand calculus, nonsmooth Tanaka formula, arbitrary-partition theorem or unbounded-target Lp convergence is claimed.
