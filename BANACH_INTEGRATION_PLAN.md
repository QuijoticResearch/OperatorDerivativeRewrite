## Blocking defects in the candidate Lean interface

1. **The core Banach structure is inconsistent.** `BanachEnergySpace` at lines 1345–1430 has `mk_dual : (H_X → ℝ) → H_X_star` and `mk_dual_spec : ∀ f u, pair (mk_dual f) u = f u`. Its `pair_smul_right` field gives `pair φ 0 = 0` by setting the scalar to zero. Put `f(u)=1`. `mk_dual_spec f 0` gives `pair (mk_dual f) 0 = 1`, hence `0=1`. This contradiction uses no nontriviality of `H_X`. All results quantified over `E : BanachEnergySpace`, including `D_def` at 1500 and factorization at 1607, are conditional on an uninhabitable structure. No `sorry` or top-level `axiom` is needed to hide a theorem behind an inconsistent structure field. The advertised derivative at 1494 is consequently **not** a constructed Banach adjoint. Repair: use the actual continuous dual `StrongDual ℝ H_X` and compose a genuine continuous Hölder functional with a bounded integral `δ`; do not map arbitrary functions to dual elements. Mathlib already supplies `ContinuousLinearMap.lpPairing` in `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Function/Holder.lean:149` for Hölder-conjugate exponents.
2. **The compensated-integral interface is also inconsistent.** `CompensatedIntegralAxioms.I₁_isometry` at 783–785 says `𝔼[(I₁ M h)^2] = norm_sq` for *every* real `norm_sq` and every functional `𝔼`. Choose `norm_sq=0` and `norm_sq=1` with the same other parameters. The same impossible universal quantification occurs in the `toAxioms` input `h_approx_iso` at 896–910. The norm must be a fixed, defined functional of `M,h` and the actual intensity; expectation must be tied to an actual probability measure. The source's own comments at 771–811 and 843–910 call major stochastic convergence and isometry properties structure assumptions. These are not constructions from finite sums.

These two contradictions were checked by Lean against a temporary, unmodified copy of the actual source. The following examples compile with the pinned toolchain; each proves that the corresponding structure is uninhabited:

```lean
example (E : BanachEnergySpace) : False := by
  have hz := E.pair_smul_right (E.mk_dual (fun _ => (1 : ℝ))) 0 (0 : E.H_X)
  have hz' : E.pair (E.mk_dual (fun _ => (1 : ℝ))) 0 = 0 := by simpa using hz
  have ho := E.mk_dual_spec (fun _ : E.H_X => (1 : ℝ)) (0 : E.H_X)
  rw [hz'] at ho
  norm_num at ho

example {Ω : Type*} (A : CompensatedIntegralAxioms Ω) : False := by
  have h := A.I₁_isometry 0 (fun _ _ => 0) (fun _ => 0) 1
  norm_num at h
```

The kernel reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` for sampled conditional declarations such as `BanachEnergySpace.D_def` and `stable_levy_obstruction`. A clean `#print axioms` result therefore cannot rescue the mathematical meaning of a theorem quantified over an impossible structure.
3. **The sign-symmetry obstruction is false for arbitrary predictable integrands.** `DistributionalSymmetry.δ_odd` at 1670 assumes `δ(u)(-L)=-δ(u)(L)` while keeping the same random predictable `u`. Path reversal transforms `u` as well. For a two-step symmetric noise with increments `ξ₁,ξ₂`, take predictable `u₂=ξ₁`; then `δ(u)=ξ₁ξ₂` is **even** under simultaneous sign reversal. Filtration equality under `L↦-L` preserves predictability, but does not make the integrand invariant. Thus `even_annihilates_im_delta` at 1700 and `stable_levy_obstruction` at 2289 do not establish the stated conclusion for the intended full predictable domain. The manuscript `C:\Operator\Banach.tex:719–788` repeats the invalid sign step at 780–788. The orthogonality claim is valid for deterministic or reversal-invariant integrands, not all predictable ones.
4. **Unrestricted products and Hilbert implications are misstated.** `LeibnizStructure` at 1845–1869 supplies total `mul_Lq : LqΩ → LqΩ → LqΩ` and `mul_qp : LqΩ → LpΩ → LpΩ` although the comments themselves say higher integrability is required. On a nonatomic probability space these are not total pointwise operations. Its `module_action` similarly assumes a terminal functional can multiply a predictable process and preserve the integrand space. Domain restrictions and Hölder estimates are required. `HilbertEnergyData.defect_zero` at 2371–2375 assumes zero jump defect; Hilbert geometry alone does not imply ordinary Leibniz. The verified one-site Poisson theory is Hilbert-valued and has `D(X²)=2X+1`.
5. **Several named theorems are conditional restatements.** `fluctuation_factorization` at 1607–1618 assumes `F ∈ range δ` and returns `δ(recovery_map F)=F` from its definition; it does not prove a representation property. `duality_recovery` at 1622–1631 does not relate its `F_Lp` argument to `F : LqΩ`; its conclusion is merely `D_def`. `derivation_iff_defect_zero` at 1890 is `Iff.rfl`. `product_rule_with_jump_defect` at 1968 uses the `LevyItoStructure` fields `ito_formula_paired` and `predictable_eq_module` at 1920–1950, which contain the substantive target decomposition. `chaos_kernel_characterization` at 2092 relies on `TruncationStructure.trunc_convergence` and `chaos_orthogonality` fields and asserts only convergence of pairings on deterministic integrands, not norm convergence of chaos coefficients. These are useful conditional algebra only after consistent concrete instances are constructed.

The *conclusion* that a single scalar stable-Lévy integral cannot represent every centered terminal functional may still be true. A safer proof for the centered large-jump count uses the unique predictable representation against the **marked compensated jump measure**: a representation by `∫u_t dL_t` would require the mark-dependent kernel `u_t z` to equal `1_{|z|>1}` almost everywhere in `(t,z,ω)`. A predictable scalar `u_t` cannot satisfy that identity for both small and large jumps of a stable Lévy measure. This needs a rigorous jump-measure representation/uniqueness theorem and the correct `L^p` or local-martingale integrability hypotheses. It is a proposed proof architecture, **not a result established in Lean here**. Classical point-process and Lévy representation context appears in [Chou–Meyer (1975)](https://www.numdam.org/item/SPS_1975__9__226_0/) and [Nualart–Schoutens (2000)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304414900000351). A concrete [Poisson martingale representation paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.3972) treats marked measures and Kunita–Watanabe decompositions. The candidate's current `δ_odd` route cannot substitute for these results.
