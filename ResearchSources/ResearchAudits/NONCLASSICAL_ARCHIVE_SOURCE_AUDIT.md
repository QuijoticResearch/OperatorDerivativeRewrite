## Blocking observations to retain in campaign planning

1. **Covariance is not freely renormalizable.** In `OperatorDerivative.tex:491,495,624`, kₜ is the covariance representer and D Xₜ=kₜ; hence ‖kₜ‖²=Var(Xₜ). For standard fBM this is t^(2H). The different value 2H t^(2H−1) at line 639 cannot be obtained while preserving those definitions. Even for Brownian motion, ‖D Bₛ‖²=s, so the literal time integral at line 771 is t²/2, not t. A variance derivative or diagonal time integrand is a different object.

2. **Complex Γ₂ needs symmetrization in the definition.** For γ=1, f(x)=e^(ix)+i e^(2ix), at x=0 the source's Γ(f,Lf)=−5+i and LΓ(f,f)=0. Its literal definition gives Γ₂=5−i. The Hadamard-square expression gives 5. Replacing `2 Γ(f,Lf)` by `Γ(Lf,f)+Γ(f,Lf)` repairs this discrepancy but changes the stated definition and must be explicit. This is a hand calculation, not a Lean-verified counterexample.

3. **Do not infer broad curvature from numerical matrices.** The Z-matrix hypothesis at `bakry_emery_revised_v8 (2).tex:790–819` remains unproved; `hurst_curvature.tex:844–898` labels a numerical finiteness finding a proposition while acknowledging the missing uniform far-field bound. The nonlocal generator also lacks the diffusion chain rule needed for a direct Gaussian log-Sobolev transfer.

4. **A concrete SPDE endpoint failure.** At α=2, E₂(−r)=cos(√r). Taking r=(2πn)² contradicts any bound C/(1+r). The series gives E₂,₂(−r)=sin(√r)/√r, which likewise lacks the stated O(1/r) bound. The manuscript itself limits the asymptotic it cites to α<2. No archive edit was made.

5. **Spatial Hilbert–Schmidt obstruction on the whole line.** A nonzero translation-invariant convolution on L²(R) has kernel k(x−y); if k∈L² and k≠0, its squared kernel integral on R² is infinite. Thus the integral of the Fourier multiplier's square in `spde/main(20261012-204518).tex:469–471` is not the global Hilbert–Schmidt norm. A bounded/periodic domain, localized observation, weighted space, or actual trace-class covariance requires a separately stated model. This is a mathematical obstruction, not merely a missing Lean lemma.

6. **Time regularity is not free.** A scalar kernel r^(α−1) has variance integral t^(2α−1)/(2α−1), so near α=1/2 one cannot infer the manuscript's almost-1/2 temporal exponent from that kernel estimate. The deterministic L² initial datum also has no asserted generator-domain regularity to absorb a frequency factor. Pointwise existence does not establish the stated path-space theorem.

7. **IBM compression is conditional on density and observability.** `EnergyFirst.tex` explicitly assumes the observable pullback domain dense. Its mollification argument shows source differentiation but does not show that the mollified functional is measurable with respect to the original IBM path. Neither statement should be encoded as an automatically satisfied structure field.

8. **Do not assemble incompatible IBM limits.** The flagship separates a first-chaos H⁻¹ dual limit, possible divergence of ordinary L² norms, and an unresolved global correction space. The expectation PDE lives in the total mean sector; positive scenery-chaos sectors have zero scenery mean. Preserve the distinction between X(Y) and X(|Y|) in all kernel/local-time conventions.

9. **Some ancillary claims need elementary corrections.** For retention, use a supremum/crossover rather than a maximum of a strict inequality; integer cutoffs require attention when the crossover is an integer. For fixed relevance ratio, λ→0 does not force λτ^α→∞ at the crossing, so the displayed large-argument Mittag-Leffler coefficient is not established by small λ alone. For the score paper, equality of precision-weighted and ordinary means means zero conditional covariance with inverse clock, not conditional independence.

10. **Earlier operator proof gaps still apply.** `OperatorDerivative.tex:810` again uses L²×L² as if it were L²; line 806 uses ambient density where graph control is needed; line 838's exponent comparison requires H≥1/2 and does not follow from γ≥H. In the Itô proof, a terminal conditional integrand is replaced by a running adapted integrand at line 869 without an identity proving that replacement. These are not discharged by the new archive material.
